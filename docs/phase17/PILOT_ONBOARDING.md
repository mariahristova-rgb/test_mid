# Controlled pilot checklist

No external customer enrolled. Decision **PILOT_BLOCKED** until staging, Calendar, email, security and attended UX gates have evidence.

1. Owner authorizes one tenant/customer and approved public knowledge, retention and escalation contact.
2. Configure assistant and per-agent tools; connect tenant-owned OAuth without importing another tenant's tokens.
3. Pass fresh BG/EN tests, verify domain ownership, generate the existing one-script loader and stage on approved HTTPS origins.
4. Verify availability/ownership/confirmation/reconciliation, mail status, dashboard and disable/recovery.
5. Owner approves per-capability publication, observes the journey and records customer feedback.

Voice, WhatsApp/SMS and payments stay disabled. Existing widget pause/revoke and origin removal are the rollback controls; preserve business records and do not cancel real events without authorization. Support levels are proposed operational targets, not measured SLA. Privacy notices need owner/legal review; no GDPR certification is asserted.
