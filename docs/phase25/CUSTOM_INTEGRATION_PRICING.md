# Custom integration pricing — proposed, not activated

User-updated Phase 25 Section 16 takes precedence over earlier proposed maintenance prices. Commercial approval is still required. No billing or payment collection is authorized.

| Tier | Scope | One-time setup | Monthly maintenance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Simple | Limited REST API; few objects/fields; one-directional workflow | €500–€1,500 | **€99/month** |
| Advanced | Multiple objects/workflows; complex mapping; bidirectional synchronization | €1,500–€5,000 | **€199/month** |
| Enterprise | Complex CRM/ERP or legacy systems; advanced security/reliability; multiple environments/custom SLAs | €5,000+ | **Custom quote** |

Monthly maintenance is separate from the customer's Ontelyx SaaS subscription. These are proposed prices, not activated offers, invoices or payment terms.

Proposed quote workflow: capture business requirements, provider/authentication, approved objects/actions, mappings, data sensitivity, environments, reliability/SLA, estimated effort, setup fee and separate maintenance fee. Human commercial approval precedes any offer. Technical acceptance specifies fixtures, security, ownership, duplicate/unknown recovery and rollback. Complex integrations are optional services; no arbitrary URL execution, customer code or real credentials are collected under the current local scope.
