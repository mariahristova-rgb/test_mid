# Health and recovery

Redacted connection states/failure codes are persisted in existing operations_events and shown on local cards. OAuth expiry requires reauthorization; denied/failed provider calls degrade the fixture; unknown writes block test/reconnect/preview. Pending receipts from an interrupted process also cannot replay. No blind retry.

Owner can disconnect to clear fake credentials while keeping unknown receipt and local history. No delete action clears evidence. Current unknown remains unresolved until a future reviewed reconciliation contract proves provider state; this service does not fabricate recovery success.

Disable via the default create_app(connector_fixtures=False), or omit the explicit flag. Strict staging/production does not enable fixtures even with the flag. Stop the named loopback fixture to end a local review. Do not extend cloud deadlines or change unrelated services. Production revocation, webhooks and background-job cancellation are unverified external gates.
