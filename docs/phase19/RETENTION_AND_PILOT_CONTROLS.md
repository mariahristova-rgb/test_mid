# Retention, cleanup and proposed pilot controls

Proposal only. Pilot owner/incident contact/participants/accounts/start-end dates/spend/capability enablement are NOT_SET. No external invitation or account activation. Initial capability recommendation: approved public FAQ; booking/email require independent gates. Voice/SIP/WhatsApp/SMS/payments disabled.

Before activation record owner approval, domain/tenant/OAuth ownership, recipients/time windows, privacy notices/retention, support/contact permissions, stop/rollback criteria and budget. Existing host deadline cannot be extended or treated as permanent hosting. Stop immediately for unauthorized action, tenant breach, duplicate write, misdelivery or critical security failure.

Cleanup checks: consented clear and bounded retention erase messages/provider/agent/tool copies and booking_state; withdraw optional journey tracking; bookings/business/financial/audit records have separate retention. Explicit broader privacy request remains tenant-admin controlled. Revoke widget/visitor authority and disconnect authorized integrations when approved; do not cancel real events/delete customer records automatically. Upstream erasure and legal retention need owner/legal review.

Validated locally: clear-after-revocation, trace/memory redaction, optional consent withdrawal, no cross-tenant cleanup, preserved appointments, encrypted isolated restore. Not executed: actual customer cleanup, provider erasure, remote backup or secret rotation.
